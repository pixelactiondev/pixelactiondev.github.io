Um Anzeigen auszuliefern und zu messen, Betrug zu verhindern, die Häufigkeit von Anzeigen zu begrenzen und – nur mit deiner Einwilligung – Anzeigen nach deinen Interessen auszuwählen.

Von Google Play erhalten wir nur zusammengefasste Statistiken (zum Beispiel Zahl der Installationen) und, falls du in Android das Teilen von Diagnosedaten erlaubt hast, anonyme Absturzberichte. Daraus können wir dich nicht identifizieren.

Google LLC) – nach den Bedingungen und der Datenschutzerklärung von Google. Deine Zahlungsdaten, zum Beispiel Kartennummer oder Kontodaten, erhalten wir nicht.

Diese Angaben werden nur auf deinem Gerät verarbeitet. Im Spielstand wird zu jedem Kauf nur eine verkürzte, nicht umkehrbare Kennung gespeichert, damit derselbe Kauf nicht doppelt gutgeschrieben wird.

Rechtsgrundlage ist die Erfüllung des Kaufvertrags und unsere gesetzlichen Pflichten (Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. b und c DSGVO).

2. Data on your device

Save data

Progress, inventory, settings and statistics are stored only in the app's storage on your device. They are not sent to us. You can remove them at any time in the settings under “Delete progress” or by uninstalling the app.

Exporting your save

“Export save” creates a code that you copy yourself to move your progress to another device. The code is never sent automatically; you decide where to keep it.

Notifications

If you allow it, the game reminds you with local notifications, for example when the AFK watch is full. They are scheduled on your device without any server. You can turn them off in the game's settings or in Android.

3. Advertising with Google AdMob

We show optional rewarded videos and occasional interstitial ads. The provider is Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland (for users in the European Economic Area and Switzerland), otherwise Google LLC, 1600 Amphitheatre Parkway, Mountain View, CA 94043, USA.

What data does Google process?

  • your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID)
  • your IP address and an approximate location derived from it (country, region)
  • device and app information such as device model, Android version, language and app version
  • interactions with ads, for example views, clicks and whether a video was watched to the end

Why?

To deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud, limit how often ads are shown and – only with your consent – choose ads based on your interests.

Consent

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, we ask for your consent via Google's consent dialog (User Messaging Platform) on first launch, before any ad is requested. Without consent you receive non-personalized or limited ads. You can change your choice in the game at any time: Settings → Privacy → “Change ad consent”. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in the Android settings.

The legal basis for personalized ads and for storing and reading information on your device is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). Non-personalized ads are based on our legitimate interest in funding the free game (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Transfers to the USA

Google may transfer data to the USA. Google LLC is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and additionally uses the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

More information: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

4. Google Play and in-app purchases

You download the game through Google Play, where Google processes data under its own privacy policy. From Google Play we only receive aggregated statistics (such as the number of installs) and, if you allowed Android to share diagnostic data, anonymous crash reports. These do not allow us to identify you.

In-app purchases

In the game you can optionally buy crystal packs, a starter bundle and “No ads”. Purchase, payment and refunds are handled by Google Play (Google Ireland Limited or Google LLC) under Google's terms and privacy policy. We never receive your payment details, such as card or bank account numbers.

So that a purchase can be added to your game and restored on a new device, the app asks Google Play for your purchases in this game (product, time of purchase, purchase identifier and status). This information is only processed on your device. For each purchase, the save data only keeps a shortened, irreversible identifier so the same purchase is never credited twice.

For billing, refunds and taxes, Google provides us with order data in the Google Play Console (such as order number, product, price, date and country). We only use it for these purposes and to help you with questions about a purchase. The legal basis is the performance of the purchase contract and our legal obligations (Art. 6(1)(b) and (c) GDPR).

5. Children

Shardfall is intended for people aged 13 and older and is not designed for children. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. Ads are limited to content suitable for teenagers.

6. Retention

Data on your device remains until you delete it or uninstall the app. How long Google keeps data is governed by Google's privacy policy. We keep order data for in-app purchases as long as tax and commercial law require. If you email us, we only use your message to reply and delete it when it is no longer needed.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, and you can withdraw your consent at any time. You may also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. Users in Türkiye have the rights under Article 11 of Law No. 6698 (KVKK).

As we do not store any personal data about you apart from order data for in-app purchases, please also contact Google directly about advertising and payment data. For anything else, write to us at pixelactiondev@gmail.com.

8. Changes

If the game changes, for example through an online leaderboard, we will update this policy. The current version is always available on this page.
